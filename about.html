About Old Indiana Map

Indiana Map began in the late 1990s as a collaborative effort led by the Indiana Geographic Information Council. The Indiana Geological and Water Survey (IGWS) built and hosted the interactive viewer, first to support planning for Interstate 69 in southwestern Indiana. Over the next two decades it grew into a statewide collection of hundreds of layers contributed by federal, state, and local partners.

By the late 2000s the IGWS was maintaining the website to serve the data. Staffing and funding for that work became increasingly difficult. In May 2022 the IGWS and the Indiana Geographic Information Office (IGIO) agreed to transfer management of Indiana Map to the IGIO. Layers began moving off the old maps.indiana.edu site in 2023; the migration was completed in 2024. The familiar custom viewer was replaced by Esri-based tools on indianamap.org.

Moving the data to a commercial platform reduced the maintenance burden and let the IGWS concentrate on geologic work. What disappeared with the old viewer was a simple, no-account tool that remembered the layers you had turned on and let you click anywhere for immediate results.

Old Indiana Map is an independent project that recreates that experience. It is a straightforward map viewer for public Indiana data. There is no login. Layers you turn on stay on when you return. Clicking the map shows the attributes of every active layer at that point. The interface is deliberately close to the original so the tool feels familiar.

Purpose

Old Indiana Map is an engineering-geology tool that brings together public layers from Indiana Map, INDOT, USGS, USDA, and related sources. It exists so practitioners can work with the original spatial data (bedrock, surficial geology, parent material, borehole records, and related coverages) and apply their own interpretation. The goal is to support the use of judgment that reduces uncertainty, cost, and risk, rather than substituting standardized data reports or agency criteria for that work.

Old Indiana Map Features

  • No account required
  • Layers and basemap remembered in your browser
  • Click-to-identify for every active layer
  • Transparency, reorder, and remove controls
  • Address and lat/lon search
  • Ability to add your own .kmz
